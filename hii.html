Symptom Checker

  • Common Cold: Drink warm fluids, get plenty of rest, gargle with salt water.
  • Minor Cuts: Clean the wound with soap and water, apply an antiseptic, and cover with a bandage.
  • Insect Bites: Wash the area and apply a cold compress to reduce swelling and itching.
  • Fever: See a doctor if a fever lasts more than 3 days or is above 103°F (39.4°C).
  • Persistent Pain: Consult a doctor if pain is severe, constant, or not relieved by over-the-counter medication.
  • Allergic Reactions: Seek immediate medical attention for severe reactions (difficulty breathing, swelling of the face).
  • Hydration: Stay hydrated by drinking water throughout the day.
  • Rest: Ensure you get adequate sleep to support your immune system.
  • Diet Tips: Eat a balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, and whole grains.
  • Pain Relief: OTC options include Paracetamol or Ibuprofen for mild to moderate pain. Always follow dosage instructions.
  • Cough Suppressants: Use non-prescription cough syrups as directed.

Vaccine Information

Vaccines are a safe and effective way to build immunity and protect against infectious diseases. They work by training your immune system to recognize and fight off specific germs without you getting sick.

Vaccination not only protects the vaccinated person but also helps prevent the spread of diseases to family, friends, and the community, a concept known as herd immunity.
